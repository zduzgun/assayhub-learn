## Issues in the Turkish source (not fixed in the English)
1. **"Almost every pose" vs "all poses" (#156 vs #209, #76).** The Lab 3 feedback says that with alignment "neredeyse her poz “başarılı” görünür", but the data give 198/198 poses under 2 Å after alignment (max 0.67 Å), and quiz Q3 says "198 pozun hepsi". Suggest "her poz" in #156.
2. **Ile16 N-terminus, "Meeko varsayılanı (tek H)" (#135/#136).** In active trypsin the Ile16 α-amine is protonated (NH3⁺) and forms the Ile16–Asp194 salt bridge that holds the active conformation. A single H is chemically wrong (a template artifact), not an equally valid alternative. It is harmless here (Vina has no electrostatics, and Ile16 is far from S1), and the report lists "N-terminus protonation review" as not run. The option text could still say that NH3⁺ is the chemically expected state.
3. **Illogical final quiz sentence (#218).** "Kaydedilmeyen ya da koşulmayan her sınav raporda açıkça yazılmalıdır": a test that was "not recorded" cannot be reported as such. The intended meaning is probably "koşulmayan ya da sonucu kaydedilmeyen her sınav". Translated literally.
4. **The two "not run" lists differ (#101 vs #195).** The "Bu sayfa hakkında" paragraph lists cross-docking, protonation review, occupancy-0 sensitivity and other scoring functions. The report card also lists other crystal waters and MD stability. This is not an error, but the two lists should match or #101 should say "among others".
5. **Wording in #148.** "yalnız atom tiplerinden H-bağı vericisi ve alıcısı olarak etkilenir" is correct in outline. For ASH the actual change is that OD2 becomes a donor–acceptor. The score is unchanged because the amidinium N atoms are donors, and OD1/OD2 stay acceptors in both states. A half-sentence saying so would make the argument complete. Not an error.
