## Issues in the Turkish source (for the author)

1. Fig. DK.1 label (pair 21): `C₇H₈N₂; amidin ucu pozitif yüklü`. C₇H₈N₂ is neutral benzamidine; the positively charged amidinium form bound to Asp189 is C₇H₉N₂⁺. Give the cation formula, or write "C₇H₈N₂ (nötr); cepte protonlanmış amidinyum".
2. Pair 39: "Hız için Vina ve AutoDock'un yaptığı gibi, reseptörün katkısı önce 0,25 Å aralıklı bir ızgarada hesaplanır" can be read as Vina/AutoDock using a 0.25 Å grid; their default spacing is 0.375 Å. Only the toy uses 0.25 Å. Suggest separating the two ("bu sayfada 0,25 Å; AutoDock'ta varsayılan 0,375 Å").
3. Salt bridge vs. Vina terms (pairs 38, 207, 216): a Vina-type function has no electrostatic term, so the amidinium–Asp189 "tuz köprüsü" is rewarded only through the H-bond (and steric) terms. The page is consistent (bar label "H-bağı / tuz köp."), but one explicit sentence would stop students concluding that Vina models charge–charge attraction.
4. Pair 90: "Arka plandaki renk, her konumda bütün yönelimler içinde bulunabilecek en iyi skordur." The code (`landscape`) minimizes over orientation θ and the tail torsion τ; "bütün yönelimler ve burulma açıları içinde" would be exact. (English kept faithful to the Turkish.)
5. Pair 132: "tek bir MD koşusunu denge diye sunmaya benzer" is a loose analogy (the usual error is presenting one short run as converged/equilibrium sampling). Consider "tek bir kısa MD koşusunu yakınsamış örnekleme diye sunmaya benzer".
6. Pair 145: "Sonra kutuları bir bir değiştirip" uses "kutu" both for the per-pose success checkboxes and for the two method toggles (symmetry, alignment) in the same task. Naming the latter ("simetri ve hizalama ayarlarını") removes the ambiguity; the English now says "the two settings".
7. Code-level plural (not a translation issue): the MD-slider aria text (pair 202) reads "1 nanoseconds" in English at t = 1 ns; `tfmt()` has the same limitation (worked around with unit symbols). A small plural helper in the source would allow full words.
