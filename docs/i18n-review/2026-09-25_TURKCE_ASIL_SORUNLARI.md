## DK-T
1. Fig. DK.1 label (pair 21): `C₇H₈N₂; amidin ucu pozitif yüklü`. C₇H₈N₂ is neutral benzamidine; the positively charged amidinium form bound to Asp189 is C₇H₉N₂⁺. Give the cation formula, or write "C₇H₈N₂ (nötr); cepte protonlanmış amidinyum".
2. Pair 39: "Hız için Vina ve AutoDock'un yaptığı gibi, reseptörün katkısı önce 0,25 Å aralıklı bir ızgarada hesaplanır" can be read as Vina/AutoDock using a 0.25 Å grid; their default spacing is 0.375 Å. Only the toy uses 0.25 Å. Suggest separating the two ("bu sayfada 0,25 Å; AutoDock'ta varsayılan 0,375 Å").
3. Salt bridge vs. Vina terms (pairs 38, 207, 216): a Vina-type function has no electrostatic term, so the amidinium–Asp189 "tuz köprüsü" is rewarded only through the H-bond (and steric) terms. The page is consistent (bar label "H-bağı / tuz köp."), but one explicit sentence would stop students concluding that Vina models charge–charge attraction.
4. Pair 90: "Arka plandaki renk, her konumda bütün yönelimler içinde bulunabilecek en iyi skordur." The code (`landscape`) minimizes over orientation θ and the tail torsion τ; "bütün yönelimler ve burulma açıları içinde" would be exact. (English kept faithful to the Turkish.)
5. Pair 132: "tek bir MD koşusunu denge diye sunmaya benzer" is a loose analogy (the usual error is presenting one short run as converged/equilibrium sampling). Consider "tek bir kısa MD koşusunu yakınsamış örnekleme diye sunmaya benzer".
6. Pair 145: "Sonra kutuları bir bir değiştirip" uses "kutu" both for the per-pose success checkboxes and for the two method toggles (symmetry, alignment) in the same task. Naming the latter ("simetri ve hizalama ayarlarını") removes the ambiguity; the English now says "the two settings".
7. Code-level plural (not a translation issue): the MD-slider aria text (pair 202) reads "1 nanoseconds" in English at t = 1 ns; `tfmt()` has the same limitation (worked around with unit symbols). A small plural helper in the source would allow full words.

This matches pairs 60 (about 0.9 Å), 146 and 277. Literature: Vina weights and the 1/(1 + 0.0585·Nrot) term, BFGS local optimization, Metropolis acceptance, exhaustiveness roughly proportional to run time (Trott & Olson 2010); Warren et al. 2006 (10 programs, 37 scoring functions); Genheden & Ryde 2015 (MM/PBSA approximations); 3PTB bovine trypsin–benzamidine, X-ray, 1.70 Å.

## DK-O
1. **"Almost every pose" vs "all poses" (#156 vs #209, #76).** The Lab 3 feedback says that with alignment "neredeyse her poz “başarılı” görünür", but the data give 198/198 poses under 2 Å after alignment (max 0.67 Å), and quiz Q3 says "198 pozun hepsi". Suggest "her poz" in #156.
2. **Ile16 N-terminus, "Meeko varsayılanı (tek H)" (#135/#136).** In active trypsin the Ile16 α-amine is protonated (NH3⁺) and forms the Ile16–Asp194 salt bridge that holds the active conformation. A single H is chemically wrong (a template artifact), not an equally valid alternative. It is harmless here (Vina has no electrostatics, and Ile16 is far from S1), and the report lists "N-terminus protonation review" as not run. The option text could still say that NH3⁺ is the chemically expected state.
3. **Illogical final quiz sentence (#218).** "Kaydedilmeyen ya da koşulmayan her sınav raporda açıkça yazılmalıdır": a test that was "not recorded" cannot be reported as such. The intended meaning is probably "koşulmayan ya da sonucu kaydedilmeyen her sınav". Translated literally.
4. **The two "not run" lists differ (#101 vs #195).** The "Bu sayfa hakkında" paragraph lists cross-docking, protonation review, occupancy-0 sensitivity and other scoring functions. The report card also lists other crystal waters and MD stability. This is not an error, but the two lists should match or #101 should say "among others".
5. **Wording in #148.** "yalnız atom tiplerinden H-bağı vericisi ve alıcısı olarak etkilenir" is correct in outline. For ASH the actual change is that OD2 becomes a donor–acceptor. The score is unchanged because the amidinium N atoms are donors, and OD1/OD2 stay acceptors in both states. A half-sentence saying so would make the argument complete. Not an error.

## DK-U
1. **Task 3, correct option (#51), "çoğu pozitif yük ve amidin taşımaz".** The natural reading, "most carry neither a positive charge nor an amidine", is chemically wrong for dopamine D2 ligands. They are almost all basic amines that are protonated (cationic) at physiological pH. The module's own data contradict it: all 54 D2 decoys have a defined `cat_asp` (distance from the *positive nitrogen* to Asp189), none has an amidine or guanidine, and none comes within 4 Å of Asp189. Suggested fix: "çoğu amidin/guanidin taşımaz ve pozitif azotları Asp189 ile tuz köprüsü kuramaz" or similar. The English follows the Turkish.
2. **"kalıntı çizgisi" (#14).** The plot draws a least-squares best-fit line (toggle "En uygun doğru"), not a residual line. Suggested fix: "en uygun doğruyu" / "regresyon doğrusunu".
3. **Section 5 (#67) promises a reference in section 6 ("kaynağını 6. kısımda vereceğiz").** Section 6 contains only the quiz, and the page has no citation anywhere. The RBFE accuracy claim is therefore unreferenced.
4. **fbD (#150-#151), "yalnız ham Vina skoru ... bu uydurulmuş ağırlıklardan daha iyi genelliyor".** The claim is hard-coded, but the numbers do not support it. On the page the fitted by-publication CV R² is 0.05 (computed in the browser) or 0.055 (precomputed, shown as 0.06 in the prose), and the raw-score baseline is 0.06. Prose D (#168) says, correctly, "aynı düzeydedir". The two statements should agree ("en az onun kadar iyi / aynı düzeyde").
5. **proseD (#167), "aynı makalenin bileşikleri eğitim ve sınamaya ayrıldığında".** Read literally, this says the compounds of one paper are *split across* training and test, which describes the leaky random split and is the opposite of grouped CV. Intended: "bileşikler eğitim ve sınamaya makaleye göre ayrıldığında" (all compounds of a paper stay on one side). The English renders the intended meaning, guided by the parenthetical "(yayına göre bölme)".
6. **Size-normalized score (Lab 2, #123-#124): only magnitudes are compared.** The panel shows r(score, pKi) = −0.36 but r(score/HA, pKi) = +0.31. Normalizing by size does more than weaken the correlation: it reverses its sign. More negative score per heavy atom goes with *lower* pKi, because larger compounds are both more potent here (r(pKi, HA) = 0.46) and less efficient. "ham skordan bile zayıf" understates this. It also sits uneasily with quiz Q2's explanation (#179), which recommends ligand efficiency. Strictly, ligand efficiency is defined from measured affinity (−ΔG/HA or 1.4·pKi/HA). It is used to compare efficiency, not to predict affinity, and "atom başına skor" is only its docking analog. One clarifying sentence would help.
7. **Enrichment factors in Lab 3 are saturated and not interpretable.** Actives form 54/82 (66 %) and 54/108 (50 %) of the two ranked sets, so EF can never exceed 1.52 or 2.0. The panel shows exactly these ceilings for EF1% and EF5%. Also, 1 % of 82 compounds is less than one compound. EF1% is meaningful only when actives are rare, as in DUD-E (about 1:50). Either add a note, or drop EF and show only AUC.
8. **"Rastgele bölmede uyum iyi görünüyor" (#61) and prose D (#166).** The random-split CV R² is 0.20. It is better than the grouped 0.05, but "iyi" oversells it. "daha iyi görünüyor" would be more accurate.
9. **"±" before RMSE (Lab 1 panel, "pKi tahmin hatası ±1,27").** RMSE is not a symmetric error bound. Suggested label: "pKi tahmin hatası (RMSE) 1,27". The "±" sits in code, not in a pair, so it is unchanged in English.
10. **"fazladan beş ağırlık" (#169) versus the CV model.** The cross-validated model (`fullCV`) fits six features (five Vina terms plus the torsion count `torsdof`) and an intercept, with ridge λ = 1. "Beş" matches the sliders but not the model whose R² is reported.
11. **"Rastgele bölme" is not random in the code.** `foldsOf` assigns groups to five folds round-robin in data order, both for the per-compound "random" split and for the by-publication split. It is an ungrouped deterministic 5-fold split. Worth one word in the text ("gruplanmamış") or a shuffled fold assignment.
12. **Publication count mismatch.** The facts bar says 37 publications (`meta.n_docs`), but the Lab 4 panel says 142 / 36 (`weight_fit.n_docs`), probably because one document has only censored values. Worth a footnote or one consistent number.
13. **Minor terminology.** "bağlanma sabiti (K<sub>i</sub>)" (#13): K<sub>i</sub> is the inhibition constant (a dissociation constant), and "bağlanma sabiti" often means an association constant. "inhibisyon sabiti (K<sub>i</sub>)" would be more precise. "EAUC" is a hybrid abbreviation; Turkish texts usually write "EAA" (eğri altındaki alan) or keep "AUC"/"ROC AUC". "ölçüt" normally means "criterion"; for DUD-E, "kıyaslama seti" or "benchmark veri seti" is clearer.

## MD-T
1. **Task 1 (Lennard-Jones) also completes on the far attractive tail, not only at the minimum.** Completion fires on `pot==='lj' && Math.abs(F) < 0.06` with F = 48 r⁻¹³ − 24 r⁻⁷ (reduced units). On the tail |F| ≈ 24 r⁻⁷ falls below 0.06 for r ≳ 400^(1/7) ≈ 2.35 σ. The slider has 1001 positions over r = 0.975–2.95 σ. Of these, 306 satisfy the condition: only one is near the minimum (slider 75, r = 1.123 σ), and 305 lie on the tail (slider 696–1000, r = 2.35–2.95 σ). I confirmed this in Chromium: dragging to r = 2.358 σ prints "Buldunuz: r ≈ 2,36 σ. Eğim burada sıfır, yani enerji eğrisinin dibindesiniz. Kuramsal değer ... ≈ 1,12 σ", which contradicts itself. The same threshold problem affects the direction readout: for r ≳ 2.75 σ (|F| ≤ 0.02) it shows "yok (denge)" (equilibrium), although the force there is weak and attractive, not zero at an equilibrium point. The minimum window is also very narrow: F′(r_min) ≈ 57 ε/σ², so |F| < 0.06 means |r − r_min| < 0.001 σ, about one slider step, which makes the intended answer hard to hit. Suggested fix: complete when |r − 2^(1/6)| < 0.02 (or |F| < tol and r < 1.5), and base the "denge" label on distance from r_min rather than on |F|.
2. **Section 3, log-scale sentence.** "bir çizgi aralığı on kat fark demektir" is wrong for this plot. The y ticks and gridlines are at 10⁻⁶, 10⁻⁴, 0,01, 1, 100 (`c3b` `yt`), so one gridline interval is a factor of **100**. Fix either the text ("yüz kat") or the ticks (one per decade).
3. **Lab 2 hint.** The hint always ends "İlk adımda hız sıfır olduğu için ortadaki terim düşer." It can be shown at any step, and from step 2 on v ≠ 0: for example, the hint for x₂ shows "(−0,1980 × 0,2)" next to that sentence. The sentence should appear only when n = 0.
4. **Pair 147: "bir proteinde yalnız ivme hesabı binlerce atom için tekrarlanır".** This is ambiguous and, read literally, inaccurate: all four update lines run for every atom, and the force/acceleration evaluation is simply the expensive part. I translated the intended meaning ("the only difference is that..."). A clearer Turkish wording would be "tek fark, ivmenin binlerce atom için hesaplanmasıdır".
5. **Minor, worth confirming against the manual.** The GROMACS page cited ("Removing fastest degrees of freedom") states the 5-steps-per-period guideline for a "Verlet (leap-frog)" scheme and rounds 13 fs / 5 to "about 3 fs". The page says "hız-Verlet ailesi" and gives 2.6 fs. Neither is wrong, since leap-frog and velocity Verlet generate the same trajectory and 2.6 fs is the unrounded value. A reader who checks the manual may notice the difference, though.
